Beyond the lab

Rower · builder · community organizer

Physics is what I do, but it isn't the whole story. Away from the cleanroom I'm usually on the water, tinkering with a side project, or helping run something that matters to the people around me.

This page is a slower, more personal look at the things I care about outside of research. It's a work in progress, much like everything worth doing.

What I get up to

On the water

Rowing

Varsity rower with several years of national-level competition. Early mornings, hard intervals, and the kind of team trust that only comes from suffering together in a boat.

Building

Side projects

I like making things — hardware, small tools, and the occasional over-engineered solution to a very minor problem. Curiosity first, polish second.

Community

Always learning
