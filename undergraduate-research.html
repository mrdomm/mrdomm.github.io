Undergraduate research

Before my master's, I worked on optics and photonics across several research groups, including Harvard's Capasso Lab and the Strickland Lab at the University of Waterloo. These projects spanned metasurface design, nonlinear optics, and optical modeling.

Selected projects

High-capacity metasurface design

Designed metasurfaces capable of producing multiple holographic outputs.

  • Large-scale FDTD simulations
  • Wavefront engineering at subwavelength scale
  • Optimization of optical response across wavelengths

Capasso Lab, Harvard

Nonlinear optical material integration

Explored nonlinear materials coupled with resonant photonic structures.

  • Investigated tunable wavelength selection mechanisms
  • Combined simulation and experimental validation

Strickland Lab, University of Waterloo

Thin film optical modeling

Built simulation tools for multilayer optical systems.

  • Transfer matrix modeling
  • Spectral response prediction
  • Comparison with experimental data
